Phone agent: customer calls (prototype)

When a customer calls the pharmacy, the agent answers basic FAQ questions so the pharmacist does not repeat them. Anything complex goes to a pharmacist with a handoff note. Sample questions only.
Ask a question to see the agent's answer.
Speed setting applies to the voice. No audio plays in this prototype.
Phone-agent gate (proposed): the rollout expands only if at least half of basic calls are answered without a human and fewer than 1 in 10 callers are misunderstood. The agent never gives clinical advice; dose, side-effect and complex questions go to a pharmacist.
